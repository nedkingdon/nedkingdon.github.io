Privacy policy

Ned Health Sync is a personal tool used only by its developer.

Data accessed: the developer's own health and sleep data (such as heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep stages), and the developer's own Google Sheet.

Use: the data is written to that Google Sheet for personal training analysis. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no other person's data is accessed.

Storage: data is held only in the developer's own Google account.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
